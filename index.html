Presentice

Rehearse your talk out loud, one sentence at a time.

Drop in your slides. Hear your script in a natural voice with every word highlighted, repeat any sentence, and say it back.

  • Stays on your device. Your slides, script and audio never leave this browser.
  • Free. No account, no limits, open source under the MIT license.
  • Voices download once. About 120 MB for Standard. High quality, if you turn it on, is about 350 MB and needs a strong graphics card.

Works best in Chrome or Edge on a laptop. Source on GitHub
